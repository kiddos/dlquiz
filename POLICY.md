## English Version

### 1. Overview and Core Principle
Driver License Quiz is an offline-first educational application designed to help users prepare for Taiwan's official Driver's License Written Exam (中華民國交通部公路局筆試題庫).

**We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).** All core features of the application operate completely offline on your device.

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal Information**: We do not require, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, address, identity number, or birth date.
- **Account & Authentication**: There are no account registrations, logins, or social profile connections.
- **Location Data**: We do not collect, access, or track your GPS or network-based location.
- **Device & Usage Identifiers**: We do not track unique hardware identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, MAC address, or IDFA/GAID).
- **Financial or Payment Information**: The application is free to use and does not process financial or payment transactions.

### 3. Local Data Storage
The application stores minimal practice data locally on your device using a local SQLite database:
- **Wrong Questions Record ("錯題本")**: When you complete a quiz simulation, questions answered incorrectly may be saved locally so you can review them later. This data includes:
  - Question ID and category (car or motorcycle, language).
  - Question content, options, correct answer, and your selected answer.
  - A local timestamp of when the question was answered.
- **Storage Location**: This data resides exclusively in the private application sandbox on your local device. It is never uploaded to any remote server or shared with third parties.

### 4. Data Control and Deletion
You retain complete control over your local data at all times:
- **In-App Deletion**: You can delete all saved wrong questions at any time by tapping the "Clear" ("清除記錄") button on the Review screen.
- **System Deletion**: You can erase all data by clearing the app's storage/data in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

### 5. Device Permissions & Network Access
- **No Sensitive Permissions**: The application does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or precise location.
- **Offline Operation**: Question banks, diagrams, and logic are bundled directly within the application package (`assets`). The app does not require internet connectivity to function.

### 6. Third-Party Services and Analytics
- **No Analytics / Telemetry SDKs**: We do not embed third-party analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics) or crash-reporting services that monitor user behavior.
- **In-App Banner**: Any promotional or informational banners displayed within the application are self-contained, static local widgets. They do not connect to third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) and do not track or profile users.

### 7. Children's Privacy
Our application is designed for users studying for driver's license exams. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, our app complies with the requirements of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

### 8. Security
Because all application data is stored solely on your device within the operating system's application sandbox, your data is protected by your device's built-in security features (such as device passcode, biometrics, and filesystem encryption).

### 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or application features. Any updates will be posted in this file and noted with a revised "Last Updated" date.

### 10. Contact Us
If you have any questions, concerns, or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, please open an issue in our project repository:
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/](https://github.com/) (or reach out via the app's official project repository page)

## 繁體中文版

### 1. 概述與核心原則
「駕照筆試題庫」（Driver License Quiz）是一款以離線使用為導向的教育模擬測驗應用程式，專為準備中華民國交通部公路局汽機車筆試之使用者所設計。

**我們絕不收集、傳輸、販售或分享您的任何個人識別資訊（PII）。** 本應用程式的所有核心模擬測驗功能均在您的裝置本機端完全離線運行。

### 2. 我們不收集的資訊
- **個人基本資料**：我們不要求亦不收集您的姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼、住址、身分證字號或出生年月日。
- **帳號與登入**：本應用程式無需註冊或登入任何帳號，亦無社群帳號連結功能。
- **地理位置資訊**：我們不會存取、追蹤或收集您的 GPS 或網路定位資訊。
- **裝置與識別碼**：我們不追蹤硬體識別碼（如 IMEI、Android ID、MAC 位址或 IDFA/GAID 廣告識別碼）。
- **財務與支付資訊**：本應用程式為免費練習工具，不涉及任何付費或金融交易資訊處理。

### 3. 本機資料儲存與使用
為了提供練習與複習功能，應用程式會透過本機 SQLite 資料庫儲存極少量的作答紀錄：
- **錯題本紀錄**：當您在測驗中答錯題目時，系統會將該題儲存於本機錯題本中，以便您日後重溫檢視。儲存內容包含：
  - 題目編號與車種類別（汽車或機車、測驗語言）。
  - 題目內容、選項、正確答案與您的作答選擇。
  - 本機作答時間戳記。
- **儲存位置**：上述資料完全保留於您裝置的本機專屬沙盒空間（Local Sandbox）中，絕不會上傳至任何遠端伺服器，亦不會與任何第三方共用。

### 4. 使用者資料控制與刪除權利
您對所有本機資料保有完全的控制權：
- **應用程式內清除**：您可隨時在「錯題本」畫面中點擊「清除記錄」按鈕，立即刪除所有已儲存的錯題紀錄。
- **系統層級刪除**：您亦可透過裝置系統設定清除應用程式儲存空間／資料，或是直接解除安裝本應用程式，本機資料將全數被徹底移除。

### 5. 系統權限與網路使用
- **無敏感權限要求**：本應用程式不索取相機、麥克風、通訊錄、相簿或精確位置等任何敏感權限。
- **離線運行**：所有題庫內容、圖片與邏輯均已內建於應用程式安裝包中（`assets`），日常練習完全不需連上網際網路。

### 6. 第三方服務與數據分析
- **無分析與追蹤 SDK**：我們未嵌入任何第三方數據分析追蹤工具（如 Google Analytics、Firebase Analytics）或行為監控 SDK。
- **廣告橫幅說明**：應用程式底部之廣告橫幅均為本地靜態元件，未串接第三方廣告網路（如 Google AdMob），不會蒐集用戶畫像或進行個人化追蹤。

### 7. 兒童隱私保護
本應用程式為公眾駕照筆試練習工具。我們不會故意收集或要求 13 歲以下兒童（或適用司法管轄區法定年齡）的任何個人資訊。由於本應用程式完全不收集任何個人資料，因此符合《兒童線上隱私保護法》（COPPA）及《通用資料保護條例》（GDPR）等法規之規範。

### 8. 資料安全
由於所有資料均僅保留在您個人裝置的作業系統沙盒內，您的資料安全性直接受到您裝置本身安全機制（如螢幕鎖定密碼、生物辨識與檔案系統加密）的完整防護。

### 9. 隱私權政策修訂
我們可能會配合法規要求或功能調整，不定時更新本隱私權政策。更新後之最新內容將直接發布於此文件中，並更新頂部之「最後更新日期」。
